MarketMaps Support

MarketMaps is an in-store aisle finder for grocery stores.

Contact us

Email carringtonrevenueops@gmail.com. We reply within two business days.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and the store you were shopping at (name and city) so we can reproduce the problem.

Which stores have in-store navigation?

MarketMaps only offers aisle-by-aisle navigation at stores where we hold that exact store's floor plan and its product-to-aisle data. Every other store is shown as unavailable. We never generate or estimate a map.

At launch that is about 2,500 stores: the Kroger family (Kroger, Ralphs, Fred Meyer, King Soopers, Harris Teeter, Fry's, QFC, Mariano's, Smith's and others), and select ShopRite, Roche Bros. and Wegmans stores.

Common questions

  • My store says "unavailable." We do not have verified data for that store yet. You can still build a shopping list for it.
  • An item is in the wrong aisle. Stores re-shelve. Email us the store, the item and the aisle you found it in, and we will correct it.
  • Is it free? Yes. Version 1 is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.
  • Accounts and my data. Version 1 has no accounts. Your lists and trips are stored only on your iPhone; deleting the app deletes them.
